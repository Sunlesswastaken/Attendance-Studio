ATTENDANCE STUDIO

1. Right-click the ZIP and choose Extract All.
2. Open the extracted folder.
3. Double-click Attendance Studio.exe. Your browser opens automatically.

Login credentials are provided separately. Ask the administrator.

For Windows 10/11 x64. No installation or internet needed.
Keep the folders beside Attendance Studio.exe; do not copy only the EXE.
An HTML file alone cannot start this local application.

Discrepancy = CDC absent and class present, for eligible CDC students only.
Absent in both or present in CDC/absent in class does not count.
